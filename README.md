## 📌 Overview
**Thuraya** is an innovative smart tourism platform designed to modernize and streamline travel experiences across Saudi Arabia. Acting as a digital bridge between travelers and local tourism businesses, Thuraya empowers users to explore authentic destinations, discover curated local experiences, and seamlessly book tourism packages through a secure, integrated payment system.

---

## 🎯 Key Features & Highlights
* **Destination Discovery:** Explore diverse cultural, historical, and modern attractions across the Kingdom.
* **Seamless Booking & Payments:** End-to-end secure online reservation process for tailored tourist packages.
* **Business-to-Consumer (B2C) Bridge:** Direct connection connecting service providers with national and international tourists.
* **Vision 2030 Alignment:** Supporting Saudi Arabia’s strategic vision to boost the national tourism sector and digital economy.

---

## 💼 Business & Revenue Model
Thuraya operates on a **commission-based business model**:
* The platform charges a percentage fee on every successful booking from the service provider side, ensuring zero upfront barrier for business partners while driving sustainable platform revenue.
